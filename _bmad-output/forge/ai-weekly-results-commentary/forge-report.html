Weekly results commentary

Pick Six · hardened 1 Nov 2026

It holds, on a budget, as a weekly note. It does not hold as an open-ended agent with a database login.

Outcome

Every league can have it. It ships off. A league admin turns it on. Kyle pays, at $1 per enabled league per week unless he sets a different ceiling on that league. The model may ask about this week or the loaded history and mention what it finds interesting. Up to 3 confirmed items. It does not pad. The app types every name and number. If nothing confirms, the line is “Quiet week. Nothing new for the record book,” and the model does not write it. The note sits under the standings and in the Tuesday digest.

Locked

  • Look across one week or the whole loaded history. Surface what seems worth saying. Three at most, and only as many as actually check out.
  • The published figure is the one the app calculated. A comparison that was not confirmed is dropped.
  • This production league gets one screenshot import, then never again. The filename is the season year, and none of the shots are the current season. Green is a win for 1. Pale blue is a win against the jailed team for 2. Red with a team is a loss for 0. An empty red cell is no pick and counts as a loss. Yellow or orange is an invalid pick for 0.
  • Go-live waits until those screenshots are in hand and every first name is mapped: a current member, or a past member who still counts.
  • New leagues do not import spreadsheets. Their book starts with their first season in the app.
  • When this league is renewed later, the imported years stay, including people who do not come back.
  • A missing year is named. The words “league history” and “all-time” wait until the set is complete. A missing year is not expected here.

Rejected, and why

  • The model types the number. A true query can still be retold as the wrong figure.
  • The model writes the quiet week. That is where it starts sounding like a status report.
  • An unbounded hunt for something interesting. It has no stop, and the bill follows the loop.
  • A league admin sets the dollar amount. That is a slider on Kyle’s invoice.
  • A general database login. Passwords and sessions live in that database.
  • A values-only CSV. The outcome is the cell color, and a CSV drops it.
  • Reading a cell by eye and trusting it. Watson’s Week 14 was read as a team in a gold cell. It is an empty red cell, a loss.
  • Spreadsheet import as a feature for later leagues. This league is the one that used to live in a workbook.

Still soft

  • “Interesting” is still the model’s taste. The cap and the check limit the damage. They do not define taste.
  • Any future league admin can turn the feature on and spend Kyle’s money, up to that league’s ceiling.
  • A new league’s “history” may be three weeks long. The sentence will be true, and it will sound bigger than the book.
  • The screenshots are the source. The import prints what it read, and a person checks that reading before any row is written. A cell whose color is ambiguous does not get a guessed team or a guessed point value. The row total is the check.
